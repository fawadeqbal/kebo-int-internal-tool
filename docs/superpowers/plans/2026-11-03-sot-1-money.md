## Changes
1. One `orderPosition(order, payments, plan, chargedExpenses)` in packages/money (order-money): total,
   charged (customer-borne expenses), settled (received + bank charges counted as paid), due = total +
   charged − settled, deposit amount taken from the payment plan's deposit row (fallback: depositPercent
   × total when there is no plan), depositReceived. Replace ALL `settle(...)` call sites (audit §3.2: 17
   places) and the SQL in money/due.ts (same rule in SQL, with a test that SQL == TS on mixed data).
   Order page header, orders list, overview, statement, payment request, receipts, reminders, reports,
   insights, checklist facts, flow gate, costs page — all show the same due. Where a charged amount exists,
   show "incl. USD 800 charged" next to due on the order page/statement/payment request.
2. One `factoryPosition(order lines, factory payments, factory-borne expenses)` (owed with tax via the
   factory cost function from #88 − paid − factory-borne expenses), used by the Money tab factory rows,
   the factory payment plan card (late/settled), ordersOwingFactories (SQL + test parity), the Costs page
   and the purchase order totals. Currency from the lines (no "CNY" fallback). PO payment sentence built
   from the FactoryPaymentPlan (not a hard-coded "balance before the goods leave the factory").
3. LC amount: bill of exchange / bank cover letter draw min(LC amount, LC balance) and the LC balance when
   the LC amount is empty (lcBalanceMinor), never the whole order total. A paper check (the existing
   checks list on the Make page) "LC amount differs from the LC balance" when both exist and differ.
4. Profit: one `orderMoney(o)` returning named figures (grossOnQuote, expected, earned, cash) used by the
   Money tab, Costs page, Reports, Insights and Leaks; leaks SQL uses BASIS_COST_CATEGORIES; same label
   for the same figure on every screen. Numbers on existing demo data may change only where the audit
   shows a formula was wrong — list every such change in the PR body.
5. The overview uses the same due whether or not the money data loaded.
